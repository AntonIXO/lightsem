## Specific research questions
1. Cleaner image training observations: distinguish actual lighting/background changes from shot cuts, camera motion, people/UI leakage and segmentation flicker. Is detector replacement necessary, or would cut awareness and temporal mask stability be the more direct intervention?
2. Richer picture-over-time representation: color-faithful spatial layout, evolving color regions, flow/composition/scene changes; frozen image/video encoders are candidates but semantic invariance or color augmentation may destroy the chromatic signal. Retain explicit color channels and test that risk. Separate temporal ordering from merely adding dimensions/capacity.
3. Spatial fixture colors: a richer target/distribution and constrained mapping from image color composition to fixed stage fixtures. First establish an oracle/output-capacity benefit before expensive audio learning. Preserve current beam geometry, cue times, dimmers, history and300ms transitions.
4. How to evaluate music fit and designed appearance on emitted/rendered light with independent labels and blinded multi-track preference, without selecting by already-exposed Shpongle or self-evaluating with the same teacher.
