## Findings

Across these four held-motion clips, all native generated arms add gestures or knee changes during source holds. New physical does not restore the reference rest/hold phrase. In these inspected displays it sometimes reduces drift or gesture amplitude versus masked, but the frames do not establish contact physics, smoothness or physical validity.

Both optical rows retain the same six-light inward-fan family across comparable world arms. Source glows and extended shafts are distinct. Native closer crops reveal small shaft direction/endpoint changes: these are not literally frozen outputs. However, much larger body-pose changes translate to modest visible spatial changes. Brightness changes are often more salient. No clear new-physical optical improvement emerged in this sampled instrument.

### holds_083_1200

- **Body:** Reference stays in a narrow place and holds one forearm overhead while the other extends/retracts. New masked and physical repeatedly lower both arms, bend knees and switch stance; neither preserves the reference hold phrase. In fast frames270-326 physical has less root drift than masked (display x1.10 to0.98 vs -0.10 to-0.40), but both cycle arm-down/crouch rather than reference arm-up. No contact/force inference. Prior is also gestural and offset; old2D moves but has uncalibrated scale.
- **original_legacy:** All world arms retain a very dark blue/cyan six-light inward fan. Brightness pulses dominate over visible shaft redirection in overviews. Prior native-width supplement has discernible diffuse shafts, not only source dots. Incumbent lights unavailable.
- **closer_zero_angular_flux:** All world arms retain six separable narrow cyan/blue inward shafts, central near vertical pair and slanted outer pair. Fast crops show small endpoint/direction changes and brightness variation, not a new layout family. Source bulbs are separate small glows; beam shafts remain clear below. Reference and both new layouts look much more alike than their body poses. No obvious new-physical optical gain.

### holds_067_0

Fast270-326 confirms knee-up to squat/recovery in both new arms, absent in reference; physical stays nearer origin than masked, but does not restore held posture. Old2D largely frontal torso with arm changes, uncalibrated.
- **original_legacy:** All world arms have same six-light inward fan; shafts dim first8s then brighten markedly around9-10s and hue shifts toward purple late. These are visible shafts and pools, not only bulbs. New physical not a distinct layout family or visibly better hold translation. Incumbent unavailable.
- **closer_zero_angular_flux:** Six narrow cyan/blue shafts and separate source glows throughout; later purple centers. Fast sequence reveals genuine small shifts of outer shaft endpoints in both new arms, with masked more pronounced than reference; still same fan, not frozen pixels. Common brightness rise overwhelms small body-dependent differences. Physical retains layout similarity, not a demonstrated optical correction.

### holds_078_0

- **original_legacy:** All world arms remain dark six-light inward blue/cyan fans. Later ~16/18/last samples brighten; diffuse shafts and small source bulbs visible at native supplement. No clear layout-family distinction among native models, incumbent lighting absent.

Six inward shafts retain shape; tiny endpoint and intensity changes accompany generated body changes. Both new arms retain near-identical fan family to reference and prior. This gives weak visual translation of the much larger pose differences, not optical acceptance.

Prior and both new native arms are gestural from start; they do not reproduce the prolonged rest. Fast270-326 confirms both new arms turn their upper body, raise/drop a forearm, cross legs and dip while reference remains almost unchanged.
