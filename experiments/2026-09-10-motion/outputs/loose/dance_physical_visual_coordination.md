## Scope limits

- Compare arms within each row. Cameras, zoom, flux and cone-edge changes across rows are presentation changes, not model gains. These are not globally brightness-matched preference tests.
- Direct audio dynamics are enabled identically in policy across arms. Body-region intensity effects also remain. A shared brightening or apparent beat response does not demonstrate music conditioning. The independent numerical wrong-audio gate is the relevant conditioning instrument.
- Old2D uses a historical uncalibrated frontal decode. Its matched world-lighting comparison is unavailable, not zero-quality lighting. The requested incumbent-lighting comparison remains unmet.
- The post-result support repair masks unsupported old2D timestamps and displays unavailability. It does not change the planned windows, models, stage mapping, or optics. This audit did not choose new windows, seeds, checkpoints, or models.
- Fixed body cameras expose position offsets but do not certify contact physics. Discrete samples do not establish continuous motion smoothness. A more restrained result is not automatically a physical-quality improvement.
- No GPU, vault, production code, TEST, or model evaluation was used by this audit. FFmpeg/FFprobe used CPU/software decode with two threads. No reviewer stage was called and no file was written inside either visual-review directory.
- Visual gate, human audiovisual acceptance, pipeline qualification, hardware safety, seed1 authorization and default promotion are not established.
