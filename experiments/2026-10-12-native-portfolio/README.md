# Native baseline body-to-light portfolio — October 12, 2026

This campaign rebuilds the 15 current body-to-light mappings on independent
Shpongle and AURORA — Cure For Me native bodies generated from the user-selected
dual-cross-attention stereo native-C seed-0 baseline in
`configs/dance/fixed_c.json`. Frozen MuQ layer 9, the authenticated 128-D
SpaIn/oBSRNN context, P2 color, restored full-song LUFS brightness, 8 beams,
5 simulated lasers and 50 bulbs are pinned in the campaign plan.

The rejected v8 artifact remains preserved for comparison. The corrected
artifact root is
`/mnt/lightsem-data/experiments/lightsem/native_portfolio_bone_preserving_20261012_v11`.
Its review index is `review/index.html`; the 30 media files are under
`media/<algorithm>/<song>/dark.mp4`.

The corrective campaign uses a bone-preserving, per-joint angular amplitude
adapter calibrated against D7_reference and a bounded soft mapper readout. It
does not stretch Cartesian bones, add an independent clock, use per-output
post-map gains, or enable D4/D6/fixture caps. All 30 saved raw bodies passed
finite, speed, acceleration, jerk, support, bone-length, per-joint angular,
and source-dependence checks before mapping. All 30 decoded mapper pairs passed
per-axis/per-direction angular readability, saturation, speed/acceleration,
active-output, and target-to-DMX checks before rendering. The 30 smoke receipts,
30 renders, and 30 full software A/V/PCM audits are recorded in
`records/DELIVERY_RECEIPT.json`.

The batch is an engineering diagnostic, not a qualified product baseline.
The Cure For Me D7_reference body was generated with a historical audio hash
(`79fbcf…`) that differs from the current Cure input (`5363ce…`), so D7 is used
only as a declared spatial/amplitude working reference. Brightness, RGB,
inactive streams and duplicate fixture commands remain separate from motion;
no artistic winner or choreography qualification is claimed.

Revisions v1–v7 are preserved as failure diagnostics: sidecar-loader mismatch,
full-song P2 crop contract, P2 activity unpacking, LUFS dictionary handling,
Chrome graphics-process admission, and historical mono Cure reference mismatch.
No failed revision was overwritten or promoted.
