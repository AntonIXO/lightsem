How is the reported error measured?

The training comparison uses recorded Just Dance coaches, not a guessed ideal dance for Shpongle or Cure. Generated motion is projected to 2D; hip translation is removed and positions are normalized by torso length. After a 50 ms measurement filter, regional speed profiles are compared at 0.2, 1 and 4 seconds using mean absolute log(1+generated speed) − log(1+recorded speed), averaged over valid observations and equally across songs.

The 32–33% figure is a reduction in that observed-cell speed-profile error, not a percentage of musical correctness. Missing region/scale cells in 38/72 windows prevent complete-primary qualification. Sliding, jerk, diversity, input sensitivity and requested-to-emitted transfer are separate checks.

The video curves show actual generated 3D speeds in m/s and motor speeds in degrees/s. Their 250 ms display average does not alter the body or DMX and is not the validation error calculation. There is no ground-truth showcase choreography, so no per-frame musical-error curve is claimed.
