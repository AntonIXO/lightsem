# Just Dance admission audit — 2026-10-07

This is a read-only admission/extraction audit. It did not retrain, change the selected native C/HF16 baseline, move a held-out group, or modify a sealed experiment. The complete machine-readable artifact is [native_admission_audit_20261007_v1](</mnt/lightsem-data/experiments/lightsem/native_admission_audit_20261007_v1/REPORT.json>).

The supplied files do not reproduce the stated “316 absent recordings” count. The legacy map has 338 rows (six marked silent); Stage11 has 669 non-silent recordings, of which 337 IDs are absent from the legacy map. Stage11 assigns those 337 as 232 TRAIN, 54 VAL, and 51 TEST. The natural 316 subset obtained by removing the 19 new records with no qualified coach and two with missing/short fingerprints is 211/54/51, so it does not produce 214/53/49. I preserved the Stage11 identities and protected all 80 Stage11 VAL/TEST canonical groups plus the 24 existing native VAL groups (104 protected groups in the union).

The saved legacy-map audit contains 994 coach tracks with an eligible 16-second masked window. Only 538 pass the final whole-record 80% gate; 456 window-eligible tracks are lost, representing 1,788.64 attached source minutes (868.25 minutes after choosing one best coach per group). Re-running the checks over all Stage11 poses gives 1,032 fixed-window tracks; 556 fail the whole-record gate, 2,177.58 attached minutes, or 225 groups / 872.23 minutes after one-coach-per-group reduction. The strict final-builder subset in the new manifest is 153 TRAIN groups (583.58 minutes). A separate 137 TRAIN groups have a valid masked window but remain below the whole-record gate; they are labeled window-only and are not promoted as strict builder data.

The pose gates are observation gates, not choreography judgments. The audit keeps confidence, box-size, torso confidence/length, hip validity, interpolation gaps, and tracker jumps separate. Of 1,076 coach tracks, three fail the reliable-torso minimum. One row has 2,670 timestamp gaps above 60 ms (maximum 67 ms); the other 1,075 have regular pose timestamps. The raw jump calculation flags 304,883 edges, but 158,897 are transitions into or out of missing keypoints. Restricting jump rejection to finite-to-finite transitions leaves 145,986 actual candidate jumps and recovers five tracks with an eligible window. The repair is tested in `tests/test_justdance_admission.py`; it does not fill missing observations or average coaches.

The audio/pose binding failure is also a policy bug. Twelve tracks (eight recordings) have a usable masked window but exceed the old 0.25-second audio-to-last-pose check. Their decoded video frame endpoint is present; the excess is an audio-container tail (45.99 attached minutes), not evidence that the pose extractor stopped mid-dance. The new manifest records the audio tail explicitly and binds pose coverage to the decoded video endpoint. It does not invent tail motion.

The Stage11 feature loop had a separate confirmed bug: an exception in one coach aborted the entire recording before later coaches were checked. `_extract_coach_tracks` now catches failures per coach, preserves later independently tracked coaches, and records `coach_failures`; `tests/test_stage11_coach_loop.py` covers this behavior. No coaches are merged or averaged.

The source-audio level manifest is stale for five of the six old exclusions. A fresh decode finds signal in `eKBQrv_wZnQ` (-26.9 dB), `8jn_f5tVhR8` (-24.4 dB), `0TuY02X_j3o` (-34.9 dB), and `R2XbWQPhuwA` (-35.0 dB), despite cached -91 dB values. `zWnvxM5cs8g` is genuinely silent. Jump In The Line (`-G-6DNPGEfk`) has usable nonzero audio at fresh mean -42.8 dB and peak -24.7 dB; a documented gain to -24 dB would put its peak near -5.9 dB, so normalization is technically safe but still requires explicit re-extraction. Those five sources total 21.64 minutes and have no pose cache, so they are not counted as admitted groups. `refresh_manifest --reprobe-audio` is an opt-in fix for stale existing rows, tested by `tests/test_justdance_audio_probe.py`; the historical manifest was not overwritten.

The 25 GB `muq_native` cache was audited before reuse. All 567 metadata records and 2,268 layer files have the pinned OpenMuQ revision `0562a57814f6f8bbd9fdea0a25921a2fce1a841a`, MuQ 25 Hz, 30-second context, 20-second core, zero time origin, finite `(frames, 1024)` tensors, and matching per-file hashes. The cache covers 567 Stage11 recordings; 102 Stage11 recordings have no cached MuQ stream. The cache therefore cannot be called whole-dataset coverage.

The frame evidence shows the failure modes directly: score/menu frames with no dancer, UI/partial-person detections that fail torso gating, a visible dancer with missing-observation holes, the audio-tail score screen, and an observed slow/held gesture. See the [contact sheet](</mnt/lightsem-data/experiments/lightsem/native_admission_audit_20261007_v1/evidence/contact_sheet.jpg>) and the individual frames in the artifact directory. Missing observations remain masks; they are never relabeled as rests.

The new [TRAIN_MANIFEST.json](</mnt/lightsem-data/experiments/lightsem/native_admission_audit_20261007_v1/TRAIN_MANIFEST.json) is explicitly `admission_manifest_not_trained`. It contains one tracked coach per canonical group, keeps protected groups out of TRAIN, marks `whole_record_qualified_80pct`, and retains window-only candidates for diagnosis. The separate [EXCLUSION_TABLE.md](</mnt/lightsem-data/experiments/lightsem/native_admission_audit_20261007_v1/EXCLUSION_TABLE.md) and [EXCLUSION_TABLE.csv](</mnt/lightsem-data/experiments/lightsem/native_admission_audit_20261007_v1/EXCLUSION_TABLE.csv) provide recording/coach-level reasons and measurements.

This work diagnoses extraction, identity, and admission quality. It does not measure native-generator musical conditioning, representation collapse, 3-D motion quality, body-to-light transfer, or emitted DMX; those remain separate gates.
