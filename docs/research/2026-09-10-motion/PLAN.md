# Motion representation first

Accepted September 10, 2026. Music is the only playback input. An internal generated
motion sequence is now explicitly allowed. Direct audio loudness and musical
transitions may control brightness and movement intensity. Quiet passages stay
visible but restrained. These permissions supersede earlier contradictory prose;
historical experimental results and archived protocols remain unchanged.

## Ordered work

1. Correct pose diagnostic visibility: finite predictions are continuously visible;
   detected uncertainty is separate. Compare synchronized source video and models.
2. Import the official FineDance 7.7-hour public release. Retain valid quiet passages,
   audit a fixed source cohort, group music duplicates before splits, keep TEST closed.
   AIST++ optimized 3D joints are a separate source-quality reference.
3. Compare a framewise 24-channel motion encoder with a temporal continuous codec:
   64 channels at 7.5 Hz, native motion 30 Hz, 16-second crops, width 256. Try temporal
   128 only if both initial codecs fail. Decode articulated 22-joint rotations,
   horizontal root velocity/height and four contacts. Integrate root travel once.
4. Only after representation gates pass: frozen decoder, Base92 audio features,
   matched deterministic regression and conditional flow on 32-second contexts.
   Keep full-sequence noise/trajectory state and test chunk boundaries.
5. One coherent motion controls the rig with regional pair differences. Audio
   loudness controls brightness/activity. Slow composition/palette changes and
   bounded beam trajectories accompany a fixed-exposure renderer profile.

## Frozen intended gates

Representation: mean joint error <= 0.02 body height, velocity RMS error <= 0.15
TRAIN velocity RMS, four-contact macro F1 >= 0.90, active travel and kinetic energy
within 10% of reference. Inspect floor penetration, foot sliding, static periods,
full-sequence continuity and 12 fixed clips (holds, travel/turns, fast coordination).
Choose lowest latent bitrate among eligible representations. Reconstruction quality
does not establish music conditioning. Source quality is an independent prerequisite.

Music: >=5% conditional energy-score improvement for correct audio versus both
donor and within-track-shift controls; song-group bootstrap CI excludes zero.
Run these tests before enabling direct audio dynamics in the lighting mapper.

Lighting: >=60% lower quiet-intro median pan/tilt speed on Shpongle, meaningful active
motion retained, visible quiet-to-entry luminance contrast and acceptable clipping.
Check emitted DMX, projected beams and bulb-region luminance separately.

Exploration seed 0, six inference draws. Only an eligible finalist and its matched
baseline receive seed 1. Initial schedules: 20,000 codec and 30,000 music updates;
BF16, compile, two-buffer prefetch, matched sample exposure after engineering
preflight. No fixed 2–3 hour ceiling. New attempts get new identities; no hidden
extra steps, seed selection, test access or reinterpretation of past failures.

All hypotheses, before/after measurements and independent gates are recorded only
in `/root/lightsem-vault`. Results and full source/codec/audio/lighting review packs
are published here as stages finish. No passing result is assumed in this plan.
